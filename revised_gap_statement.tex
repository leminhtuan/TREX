The recently standardized x402 protocol enables pay-per-request micropayments. x402 standardizes payment authorization and, in its newer versions, may define escrow-oriented payment flows. However, it does not standardize a cryptographically bound SLA-predicate-conditioned settlement state machine in which release/refund decisions are derived from threshold-attested service evidence and bound to a canonical request/predicate/response tuple.
